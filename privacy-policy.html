Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Frameskip ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting information associated with your use of our games and services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use a Frameskip game, how that information may be used, and how third-party services integrated into our games may process information.

By downloading or using a Frameskip game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Our games do not require you to create an account or provide us directly with your name, email address, phone number, or similar contact information in order to play.

However, our games and the third-party services integrated into them may automatically collect certain technical, advertising, diagnostic, and gameplay information.

This may include:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • IP address
  • Country or general geographic region
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device or installation identifiers
  • Game version
  • Gameplay events and progress
  • Session information
  • Interactions with advertisements
  • Diagnostic and crash information
  • Performance information
  • Information related to in-app purchases
  • Achievement, leaderboard, or other gaming-service information

The exact information collected may depend on the platform, device, services enabled, and your settings.

3. How We Use Information

We and our service providers may use collected information to:

  • Operate and provide our games
  • Save and maintain game progress where applicable
  • Understand how players use our games
  • Analyze game performance and player behavior
  • Improve gameplay and game design
  • Identify and fix bugs and technical problems
  • Monitor crashes and performance
  • Provide and measure advertisements
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity
  • Process and support in-app purchases
  • Provide achievements, leaderboards, and other platform gaming features
  • Maintain the security and functionality of our games

4. Analytics

Our games may use GameAnalytics to collect and analyze gameplay and technical information.

GameAnalytics may process information such as gameplay events, session information, device identifiers, and other information associated with gameplay.

We use analytics information to understand how our games are being used, identify problems, measure performance, and improve our games.

5. Advertising

Our games may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

AdMob and its associated software development kits may process information such as IP address, advertising identifiers, device or account identifiers, app interactions, advertising interactions, diagnostics, and performance information.

This information may be used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, analytics, and related purposes.

Advertising and privacy choices may depend on your device settings, platform, location, and applicable laws.

6. In-App Purchases

Our games may offer optional in-app purchases.

Payments are processed through the applicable app store or payment platform, including Google Play, Apple's App Store, or Amazon Appstore.

Frameskip does not directly receive or store your complete payment card, bank account, or other payment credentials through the game.

The applicable app store may collect and process information necessary to complete and manage purchases according to its own privacy policy and terms.

7. Google Play Games and Apple Game Center

Our games may use platform gaming services such as Google Play Games and Apple Game Center.

These services may provide features such as:

  • Achievements
  • Leaderboards
  • Game progress
  • Player profiles
  • Other platform-specific gaming features

These services may process information associated with your gaming account, gameplay, achievements, device, or other platform information.

8. Unity Services

Our games are developed using Unity and may use Unity services to provide certain functionality.

Depending on the Unity services integrated into a particular game, Unity may process technical and device-related information such as device identifiers, IP address, device model, operating system, language, hardware information, advertising identifiers, application information, and gameplay or session information.

The specific Unity services used by a game may vary.

9. Location Information

Our games do not intentionally request or collect your precise location.

Some third-party services may be able to estimate a general geographic region from information such as an IP address.

10. Children's Privacy

Our games are intended for a general audience and are not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that violates applicable law, please contact us using the contact information below.
